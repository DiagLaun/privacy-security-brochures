# High-Risk Environments — Quick Tips / 高风险环境快速提示

A short, printable cheat-sheet for users with elevated threat models —
journalists, lawyers, activists, NGO staff, frequent border crossers, and
anyone working with sensitive sources. For the full guide, see
[`high-risk-environments`](./content/brochures/high-risk-environments.mdx)
and [`高风险环境下的应急处置`](./content/brochures/high-risk-emergency.zh.mdx).

---

## English

### Before you go

1. **Threat-model on paper.** Who would target you, what can they do, and
   what is the *one* thing you cannot afford to lose?
2. **Use a clean device.** A factory-reset travel phone or laptop with
   only the apps and accounts you'll actually need on the trip.
3. **Strong, memorable passcode.** 8+ digit PIN or a passphrase, *not* a
   pattern, *not* "1234".
4. **Disable biometrics on the move.** Face/fingerprint can be compelled
   in many jurisdictions; passcodes have stronger protection.
5. **Encrypt before you cross.** Confirm full-disk encryption is on;
   sensitive files in a separate encrypted container or E2EE cloud.
6. **Move secrets off the device.** Anything irreplaceable lives in
   end-to-end-encrypted cloud or on physical media you don't carry.
7. **2FA on hardware keys, not SMS.** SIM-swap and SMS interception are
   real. YubiKey / Titan Key / Passkeys are phishing-resistant.
8. **Practice "panic-disable biometrics" once.** iPhone: long-press side
   button + volume; Android (recent): power-button lockdown.

### At borders, checkpoints, or under questioning

9. **Power off completely** — not just lock the screen. Cold boot is the
   strongest state.
10. **Keep your story simple and don't lie.** Lying makes everything
    worse.
11. **Comply with lawful demands; do not consent to optional searches.**
    These are different things. Know the difference for your
    jurisdiction.
12. **Ask for a lawyer, in writing if possible.** Note time, place,
    names, and badge numbers.
13. **Carry a paper card** with one trusted contact + one criminal-defense
    or rights organization phone number. Phones get taken; paper doesn't.

### After contact

14. **Treat any device that left your sight as compromised.** Do not log
    in, do not connect to networks. Wipe and rebuild.
15. **Rotate the most sensitive credentials first** — email, password
    manager, cloud, messaging app — from a known-clean device.
16. **Revoke active sessions** on every important account.
17. **Verify recovery emails / phone numbers** still point to you, not to
    a number an attacker now controls.
18. **Privately warn anyone whose safety depends on your device** before
    posting publicly about what happened.

### Daily habits while in a high-risk area

19. **Use Signal with disappearing messages** for the most sensitive
    contacts; verify safety numbers in person.
20. **Don't connect work and personal accounts on the same device.**
21. **Don't use public/airport Wi-Fi for sensitive logins.** Mobile data
    is usually safer.
22. **Don't install local "support" or "diagnostic" apps** from a kiosk
    or hotel — they often install root certificates.
23. **Lockscreen notifications: hide content.** Cameras, shoulder-surfers
    and seized devices can read previews.

> **Bottom line:** match defenses to your real threat model. The most
> important things are: a clean device, encrypted communications,
> hardware-key 2FA, paper backup of legal contacts, and the discipline
> to keep work and personal life on separate devices.

## 中文（简体）

### 出发前

1. **用纸笔做威胁建模**：谁会针对你、他们能做什么、你最不能丢的是哪一样？
2. **用一台干净的设备**：出行机出厂刷机后只装真正需要的 APP 与账号。
3. **强且能记住的开机密码**：6 位以上 PIN 或一句口令，**不要图形锁、不要 1234**。
4. **路上关闭生物识别**：在多数司法管辖区，指纹/面容比密码更容易被强制要求。
5. **过境前先加密**：确认全盘加密已开启；敏感文件放进独立的加密容器或 E2EE 云。
6. **不要把秘密带在身上**：不可替代的资料只放端到端加密云或不随身的物理介质。
7. **二步验证用硬件密钥，不要短信**：SIM 卡劫持是真实风险；YubiKey、Titan、通行密钥（Passkey）抗钓鱼。
8. **提前演练"紧急禁用生物识别"**：iPhone 长按侧键+音量键进入紧急 SOS 后取消；Android 近期版本支持电源键多按进入"锁定模式"。

### 边境、检查站、被询问时

9. **彻底关机**，不是只锁屏。冷启动状态最强。
10. **故事简单、不要撒谎**，撒谎会让事情变更糟。
11. **依法配合 ≠ 自愿同意搜查**。两件事不一样，请提前了解你所在司法管辖区的边界。
12. **要求律师，能书面就书面**，记下时间、地点、对方姓名与警号。
13. **随身带一张纸条**：1 位你信任的联系人 + 1 位刑辩律师 / 数字权益机构电话。手机会被收走，纸条不会。

### 事件之后

14. **离开过你视线的设备一律视为已被植入**。不要登录账号、不要联网，抹除并重建。
15. **优先轮换最关键的凭证**：邮箱、密码管理器、云盘、即时通讯——从一台已知干净的设备操作。
16. **在每个重要账号里注销所有活动会话**。
17. **检查每个账号的"恢复邮箱 / 恢复手机号"** 仍然指向你本人，而不是已经被改成的对方号码。
18. **先私下提醒**那些"安全依赖于你这台设备"的人，再考虑是否对外公开说明经过。

### 在高风险地区的日常

19. **最敏感的联系人用 Signal，开启"消失消息"**，并当面核对安全号码。
20. **工作账号与生活账号不在同一台设备上同时登录**。
21. **不要在公共 / 机场 / 酒店 Wi-Fi 上做敏感登录**，用蜂窝数据通常更安全。
22. **不要装陌生的"网络助手 / 系统诊断" APP**——它们经常顺手安装根证书。
23. **锁屏通知改为"隐藏内容"**：摄像头、身边的人、被收走的设备都可以看到预览。

> **底线**：防御要匹配真实的威胁模型。最重要的几件事是：干净的设备、端到端加密的通讯、硬件密钥二步验证、纸质备份的法律联系人，以及把工作和生活分开在不同设备上的纪律。
